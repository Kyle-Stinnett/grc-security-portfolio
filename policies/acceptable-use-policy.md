# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## Purpose
To ensure appropriate use of organizational systems and data.

## Policy
- Users must only access systems necessary for their role
- Unauthorized access is strictly prohibited
- MFA must be used where applicable

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action, including termination.

## Framework Alignment
- ISO 27001 A.9
- SOC 2 CC6
- NIST AC
